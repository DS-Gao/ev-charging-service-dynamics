\section*{Data and code availability}
The anonymized charging data are available in the second release at DOI \url{10.5281/zenodo.15564121}; the energy-transition policy corpus is available at DOI \url{10.6084/m9.figshare.32182515.v1}. Some underlying policy texts require a PKULaw subscription; original government sources retain their separate access routes. Research code, permitted analysis inputs, complete saved estimates, sample memberships, figure-source data and manuscript sources are available in the versioned research-materials repository \citep{gao2026materials}. The repository distinguishes reproduction from saved results from estimation using the documented inputs, and identifies third-party access and licensing conditions. SI Section~\siref{sec:si-sources} describes source selection and measurement definitions.
