# Charging-field source notes

This note documents the field correspondence used by the service-dynamics
analysis. It is limited to the released operator-network replication archive,
its formal article and supplementary material, and the source scripts shipped
with the archive.

## Source and field correspondence

The analysis uses version 2 of the replication archive (Zenodo DOI
`10.5281/zenodo.15564121`), specifically `Data/ev_air.csv`. The file contains
139,612 city--week rows for 2016--2023. The formal supplementary material for
the associated article (DOI `10.1038/s44284-026-00395-2`) defines:

| Upstream name | Definition in the formal supplementary material | Released field used here | Study transformation |
|---|---|---|---|
| EVCS | Cumulative number of EV charging stations in a city as of a given week | `cumulative_plugs` | Compatible-week gate `cumulative_plugs > 0`; `E` sums the released field over those dates; `N = E/W` |
| Charge | Charging amount recorded in a city during a given week | `Total` | Source-coded zeros are retained; `Q` sums the released field over compatible dates; `A = Q/W` |
| Source date | The date attached to each city--week record | `date` | Parsed to calendar year/quarter; `W` counts compatible source dates, not distinct `week_only` labels |

The shipped `Scripts/Figure1.R`, lines 240--271, reads `Data/ev_air.csv`,
converts `Total` and `cumulative_plugs` to numeric, sums `Total` by city and year,
takes the city-year maximum of `cumulative_plugs`, and labels the
station-normalized display `EVCA per station (kWh)`. `Scripts/Table2.do` maps
`log_cumulative_plugs` to Log(#EVCS) and `log_Total` to Log(Charge). These
operations establish a field-to-concept correspondence. They do not prescribe
the same city-year maximum for this study: the present analysis uses same-date
compatible-week sums and counts.

The supplementary material reports effects per 1,000 kWh of Charge in Table
S2. This is the upstream article's reported unit for Charge; it does not prove
that the released `Total` field is untransformed or that the operator network
is a complete physical network.

The formal supplementary Table S1 displays the following moments, and the
version 2 file matches them at the displayed precision:

| Field | Mean | Standard deviation | Minimum | Maximum |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `Total` | 14,198.4 | 81,928.4 | 0 | 1,861,663 |
| `cumulative_plugs` | 49.68 | 296.87 | 0 | 5,717 |

The Table S1 header prints 139,542 observations, whereas the downloaded v2
file contains 139,612 rows. Moment agreement therefore supports the field
mapping but does not prove record-level identity, a specific transformation,
or preservation of every aggregation property.

## Released scale and unknown transformations

The article describes the operator records as desensitized under the partner's
privacy requirements. The README, archive metadata, formal article,
supplementary material and source scripts inspected for this analysis do not
state whether the released fields were subject to a common positive rescaling,
a fixed city-specific factor, or a time-varying transformation. They also do
not document how such a transformation treats zeros or cumulative values.
These are possible effects to distinguish, not assertions about the actual
release process:

* A common factor applied consistently to the activity field (`A` or `Q`)
  changes activity units but leaves the `N`-based facility weights unchanged.
* A city-specific factor applied to the facility indicator (`N` or `E`) can
  change cross-city facility levels, facility weights and facility-weighted
  network aggregates.
* Time-varying activity factors can change activity growth and intensity;
  activity-only changes leave the `N`-based facility weights unchanged, while
  time-varying facility factors can additionally change facility weights.

Activity scaling can also change aggregate intensity (`Q/E`) and the
observation contributions to a PPML pseudo-likelihood. Facility scaling can
change the facility-weighted denominator itself. City fixed effects and PPML
do not certify any of these properties, so results remain comparisons of
released, observed-network indicators rather than independently certified
physical totals or shares of the operator's complete network or the complete
public network.

The cumulative facility indicator is not interpreted as online capacity, power,
connector count, uptime or service reliability. A source-coded zero is retained
as a released record state; it is not recoded as a verified physical shutdown.

## Reproduction and citations

The complete-week observation-support check is generated by
`scripts/compare_compatible_weeks.py`. With the local v2
weekly archive, the script parses `date`, reconstructs endpoint `Q`, `E` and
`W`, verifies the result against `annual_panel_v12.csv`, and writes the fixed
member list and SI table. The annual panel alone is sufficient for the public
CLI; supplying `--weekly-csv` enables the source-date audit.

Existing manuscript citation keys are sufficient: `ma2026` cites the formal
article and `evarchive2025` cites the v2 Zenodo release. No new citation key is
required for these field notes.
