# Policy coding and timing

Policy evidence was coded at clause level and linked to document and city identifiers. The research-window corpus contains 601 locally applicable documents covering 176 cities; 207 documents in 88 cities provide 466 retained requirement clauses for the non-exclusive content descriptions. Those descriptions classify information and supervision, operation and maintenance, continuing operation or payment, and assessable or subsidy-linked conditions. The annual exposure variables use a broader rule-coded evidence set together with a separately maintained first-record registry: `R` denotes information/supervision responsibility and `S` denotes operating-service responsibility, with `S` broader than the narrow operation-and-maintenance content group. First-record exposure was set to (`1[t > first_record_year]`) only for a known qualifying record; unknown coverage or timing remained missing, and an observed corpus without a qualifying record was an observed zero. Construction-support controls were document counts assigned by the effective/publication-year rule. The policy associations use 325 city-years, 94 cities and 25 province clusters; R switches in 10 cities across 7 province clusters and S in 6 cities across 6 province clusters, with overlapping city sets possible. These variables describe documented responsibilities and conditional associations, not implementation or causal effects.

## Coding support and scope

- Content path: 284/168/78 information and supervision; 97/58/35 operation and maintenance; 15/11/11 continuing operation or payment; 47/33/19 assessable or subsidy-linked; 80/52/29 unclassified retained attributes (clauses/documents/cities).
- Broad registry path: 345/183/80 R and 132/77/43 S (clauses/documents/cities).
- Bounded checks: 220 directed first-record rows; 127 rows with completed recorded check status; 37 correction records; 7 removed clauses; a prior content check covered 58 labelled rows corresponding to 52 independent source fragments. These scopes do not estimate full-corpus accuracy.
- AI assistance was used for contextual reading of retained policy sentences against the stated rules; this does not constitute human expert or double-independent validation.

The historical machine identifiers `S_prev_observed_v9` and `policy_operation_maintenance` map to broad operating-service responsibility, including maintenance, fault response, continuity, availability, public access and service qualifications. They are distinct from the narrower operation-and-maintenance content category.
